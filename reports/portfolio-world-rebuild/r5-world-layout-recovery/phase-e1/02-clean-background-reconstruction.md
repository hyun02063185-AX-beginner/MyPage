# Clean background reconstruction

Two independent 1280×720 clean plates are present: `11-workshop-clean-background.png` and `12-hero-water-clean-background.png`. They were generated as constrained edits of Candidate B, then normalized to the C.2 source coordinate system. They are not mutations of Candidate B.

Workshop/Hall removes the Hall, Workshop, their structural stairs/rails, and attached forecourt elements, and estimates continuous stone terrace, cliff connection, and planting. Hero removes the Hero Ship, berth, gangway, piles, and their reflections, estimating continuous water and contact area.

Visual check: neither plate contains the removed Hero hull or Workshop/Hall silhouette; no obvious opaque cutout hole is visible in the review board. However, both plates contain estimated pixels where the reference held no source information. Their painterly detail and local perspective are close but not pixel-identical to Candidate B. They are suitable for this replacement experiment only, not approved production backgrounds.
