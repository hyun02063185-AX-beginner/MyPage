# Workshop / Hall asset reconstruction

`production-pilot-assets/` contains transparent, source-matched segments for the Hall facade, Hall stair/retaining wall, Workshop front, and foreground rail/planters, plus a reconstruction bundle used by `13-workshop-reconstructed-composite.png`.

The bundle is extracted from the immutable Candidate B with feathered alpha masks. It is placed at `(0,0)` above the clean base; its Workshop ground reference is C.2 `N_WORKSHOP (245,440)`. The foreground rail/planter segment is depth 70, above the 28×56 player. This preserves the original building proportion rather than inventing a new facade or staircase.

Result: there is one visible Hall/Workshop structure in the composite. The segmentation edge and estimated terrace texture remain a CONDITIONAL art issue.
