# R5 E2 — Hybrid playability pilot

This is an independent Phaser browser pilot at `portfolio-world/r5-hybrid-pilot.html`; it does not modify `WorldScene`, Canonical R4, or create an R5 runtime.

Candidate B remains one scenic plate. Collision is explicit C1+C2 geometry, never alpha or plate pixels. The only separate art is the small foreground-occluder set: Hall stair retaining, Workshop foreground, Archive approach foreground, dock piles, and gangway.
