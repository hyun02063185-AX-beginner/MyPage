# R5 Phase E — Art Production Method Comparison

| Method | Fidelity | Gameplay | Camera | Cost | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flattened scenic plate | High only while static | Low | Cannot reveal hidden art | Low | Not recommended for exploration |
| Hybrid authored layers | High after reauthoring | Medium–high | Supports depth and future edges | Medium–high | Recommended, conditional |
| Fully modular assets | High after full direction | High | Strongest | Highest | Future option |

Hybrid is preferred because it retains Candidate B’s visual direction while replacing only gameplay-critical structures. It is conditional: a source plate with alpha cutouts is not enough. Terrain, water, architecture, dock, ship, and occluders must be genuinely authored before Runtime approval.
