# Semantic asset reconstruction

`production-assets/hero_ship/group.png` is controlled as one Hero Ship group: hull, deck, rails, masts, sails, rigging, and a small internal continuity patch. `gangway/gangway.png` contains the visible plank only. Dock and contact assets are separate. Group OFF removes the group; Gangway OFF keeps the group and continuity patch.

The R2 extraction is Candidate-B-pixel-led. A generated transparent ship reference is preserved under `option-b-unapproved/` solely as an Option B comparison. It is not used in any final composite because its orientation/proportions do not faithfully match Candidate B.
