# Fourth Visitable Point + Gate C Foundation Art Upgrade

## Outcome

`CANONICAL_AMENDMENT_01_FOURTH_VISITABLE_POINT` adds a proposed fourth visitable place without replacing the approved Projection A or Blueprint. The package keeps the asymmetric Hall / Workshop / Hero pattern, adds a small Harbor Office proposal at the Lower Plaza–Central Quay join, and replaces the failed schematic Foundation art with an independently authored limestone/quay/water family. No Phaser, Graybox, collision, camera, player, runtime import, or approved polygon is changed.

## Harbor Office and asymmetry

The office is a spatial endpoint for future About, Teaching, or Contact content—not another Tier-1 landmark. Candidate A is a taller office with a maritime flag; Candidate B is a lower asymmetric office with a blue awning, notice board, lamp, and minimal barrels. **Office-B is recommended pending human selection** because its 280×205 canvas is clearly below Workshop-C and preserves the Hall/Hero hierarchy.

Placement A (`700,505`) is recommended pending human approval. It visually emerges after Workshop→Lower Plaza and allows an offset choice toward Hall or Hero rather than completing a square/cross. Placement B is the Hall-side quay alternate; C is the waterfront alternate. P8 is proposed at `(815,735)`, Level 0, 150px local walkable width, with intended connections to P2/P3/P4. The amendment records only collision intent; it implements no collision.

## Foundation and water upgrade

- **Hall / Lower Plaza:** new F1 fine irregular pale limestone courses with low-frequency tonal variation; Hall and Workshop receive soft contact shadows.
- **Stairs / terrace:** new F4 limestone risers integrate into F5 capstone-and-wall thickness at P4→P5.
- **Central / Hero Quay:** new F2 surface and F3 wall-cap family give a continuous harbor-construction language and quieter support beneath Hero-B.
- **Water:** new W1 provides calm turquoise depth, soft caustic suggestion, and small rounded ripples; Hero, Secondary, and Workboat contacts are separate soft shadow layers without foam.
- **Grounding:** each building and vessel is preceded by a soft occlusion/contact shadow; no asset is baked into the Foundation.

All replacement Foundation sources are 512-based power-of-two modules where applicable, separately owned, and reproducibly generated by `scripts/portfolio-world/build-fourth-point-foundation-upgrade.py`.
